Su learns German
Fernweh
[FERN-veh]
Wanderlust — the ache for faraway places
"Ich habe Fernweh nach den Bergen."
"I have a longing for the mountains."
🧠 Memory trick: "Fern" = far, "Weh" = pain/ache. Fernweh = the pain of feeling pulled toward somewhere far. Germans have a word for missing places you've never even been to. Iconic.
✨ Bonus: The opposite is "Heimweh" (homesickness). Use both when Alex talks about Munich vs. SP.
