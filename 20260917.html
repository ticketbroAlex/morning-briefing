🎓 Language School · Thursday = Travel Theme
🇩🇪 Su lernt Deutsch
Reisefieber
/ ˈʀaɪzə.fiːbɐ /
Travel fever — that buzzy, can't-sit-still feeling before a trip
"Ich habe schon Reisefieber, obwohl wir erst nächste Woche fliegen!" "I already have travel fever, even though we don't fly until next week!"
💡 Trick: Reise = journey. Fieber = fever. You're literally sick with excitement about traveling. Germans invented a word for this feeling because of course they did.
🎁 Bonus: "Fernweh" (fern = far, weh = ache) = longing for far-away places. Germany's version of saudade — but make it geographic.
🇧🇷 Alex aprende Português
Mochileiro
/ mo-shi-ˈle-ɾo /
Backpacker — but also anyone traveling light, spontaneously, on their own terms
"Vamos ser mochileiros e explorar o interior de SP neste fim de semana!" "Let's be backpackers and explore the SP countryside this weekend!"
💡 Trick: Mochila = backpack. Mochileiro = the person with the backpack. If you've ever gone somewhere with only a carry-on, Su would call you a mochileiro honorário.
🎁 Usage tip: "Ele é muito mochileiro" is a compliment — someone low-maintenance and adventurous. Use it when someone's being refreshingly spontaneous.
