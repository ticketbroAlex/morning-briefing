🎓 Language School — Monday: Work Vocabulary
🇩🇪 Su apprende Alemão
Feierabend
/FYE-er-ah-bend/
The sacred end of the workday. Literally: "celebration evening."
"Ich hab endlich Feierabend!"
"I'm finally off work!" — said with deep German relief 🍺
🧠 Memory trick: Germans celebrate so hard when work is over, they literally named it a party. That says everything about their relationship with Feierabend.
Bonus: "Schönen Feierabend!" = "Enjoy your evening!" — what every German says at 5pm on a Friday like it's a benediction.
🇧🇷 Alex aprende Português
Trampo
/TRAM-po/
Slang for work/job. Everyone in São Paulo uses it daily.
"Como tá o trampo hoje?"
"How's work going today?" — casual, friendly, very SP.
🧠 Memory trick: Like "tramping" through the week — feet barely keeping up. Very Monday energy. Once you use this, you officially sound Paulistano.
Bonus: "Esse trampo tá me matando" = "This job is killing me." Deploy on hard Mondays. Su will laugh.
