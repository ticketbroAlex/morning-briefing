🎓 Language School  ·  Wednesday = Emotions
🇩🇪 Su learns German
Sehnsucht
/ ZEHN-zookt /
A deep, aching longing — for a person, a place, or something you can't quite name.
"Ich habe so eine Sehnsucht nach dir, wenn du nicht da bist."
"I feel such a deep longing for you when you're not here."
Memory trick: "Seh" (to see) + "sucht" (seeking) — it's the constant searching to see someone who isn't there. Germans felt it so intensely they invented a whole word for it.
Bonus phrase: "Du fehlst mir" = literally "You are missing from me" — more emotionally loaded than just "I miss you."
🇧🇷 Alex learns Portuguese
Saudade
/ sow-DAH-ji /
A bittersweet longing for someone or something absent — can be past, present, or even imagined future.
"Estou com saudade da sua risada."
"I'm missing the sound of your laugh."
Memory trick: Think "sad-age" — the melancholy that grows with distance and time. Portuguese is so good at emotional sadness they exported this word to linguistics textbooks worldwide.
Bonus: "Que saudade!" = "I missed this so much!" — say it when you see someone after a long time, eat your favorite food, or return somewhere meaningful. Instant local credibility.
