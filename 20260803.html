🎓 Language School · Monday: Work Edition
🇩🇪 Su lernt Deutsch
Kündigung
/ˈkʏndiɡʊŋ/ · KUEN-di-goong
Termination notice / resignation letter
"Ich habe heute meine Kündigung eingereicht."
"I submitted my resignation today."
🧠 Memory trick: sounds like "cunning" + "gung-ho." Someone who's cunning and gung-ho just quit dramatically. Very German.
Bonus phrase: "Die Kündigung wurde angenommen." = "The resignation was accepted." — useful in both good and bad scenarios.
🇧🇷 Alex aprende Português
Prazo
/ˈpɾazu/ · PRAH-zoo
Deadline / term / timeframe
"O prazo para entrega é amanhã."
"The delivery deadline is tomorrow."
🧠 Memory trick: "Prazo" sounds like "prize-o" — only the one who hits the deadline gets the prize. Miss the prazo, miss the prêmio.
Usage tip: "Qual é o prazo?" = "What's the deadline?" — use this constantly in any Brazilian business meeting and you'll sound like a native operator.
