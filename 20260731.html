Schnapsidee
/SHNAPS-ee-day-uh/
🇬🇧 A crazy, brilliant-sounding scheme that only makes sense after a drink. Literally: "schnapps idea." — noun, feminine
DE: "Firma in zwei Ländern aufbauen? Das war vielleicht eine Schnapsidee — aber sie funktioniert."
EN: "Build a company in two countries? Maybe a schnapps idea — but it's working."
🧠 Memory trick: Imagine downing a Schnaps and suddenly thinking "let's open an office in São Paulo!" — that eureka moment is a Schnapsidee. Alex has several per month. 🍶
🍻 Bonus: "Das klingt nach einer Schnapsidee — ich bin dabei!" → "That sounds crazy — I'm in!"

Alex learns Brazilian Portuguese
Dar um jeitinho
/dar oom zhay-CHEEN-yoo/
🇬🇧 To find a creative workaround; to make the impossible happen through resourcefulness, charm, and Brazilian ingenuity.
PT: "A reunião foi cancelada? Sem problema — a gente dá um jeitinho, manda um WhatsApp."
EN: "Meeting cancelled? No problem — we'll find a way, just WhatsApp them."
🧠 Trick: This is Brazil's national superpower and competitive advantage. "Jeitinho" comes from "jeito" (way/path). When someone says no, Brazilians don't accept it — they find another door. Su does this daily. Watch and learn. 🚪
💡 Usage tip: When you hit a dead end in any deal or situation — "Vamos dar um jeitinho" signals: I'm not giving up, just rerouting. Instant rapport with any Brazilian.
