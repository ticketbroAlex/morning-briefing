🎓 Language School · Wednesday Theme: Emotions
🇩🇪 Su lernt Deutsch
Sehnsucht
/ ZAYN-zookht /
A deep, bittersweet longing for something distant, lost, or unattained — untranslatable in any other language.
"Ich habe Sehnsucht nach dir, wenn du nicht da bist." "I have a deep longing for you when you're not here."
🧠 Memory trick: "ZAYN-zookht" — sounds like your zen went searching for something it can't quite reach. That's exactly the feeling.
Bonus: Germans say "Fernweh" for wanderlust — literally "far-pain." The language really commits to its feelings.
🇧🇷 Alex aprende Português
Saudade
/ sow-DAH-jee /
A melancholic longing for someone or something beloved. Neither sadness nor nostalgia — something between them that only Portuguese captured.
"Estou com saudade de você." "I miss you" — but deeper than that, more like: "you are absent and I feel it in my whole body."
🧠 Memory trick: "Sow-dad-jee" — the sadness your soul decides to keep, because even missing someone beautiful feels like a gift.
Dica bônus: "Tô com saudade" is how you actually say it day-to-day. Su will melt if you use it correctly. Don't rush it — earn it.
