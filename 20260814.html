🎓 Language School · Friday Edition — Fun & Slang

Su learns German
FEIERABEND
/FYE-er-ah-bend/
End of the working day · Quitting time · "It's finally over."
"Endlich Feierabend!" = "Finally, it's quitting time!"
"Wir haben Feierabend — lass uns feiern." = "Work's done — let's celebrate."
🧠 Memory trick: Feier = celebration + Abend = evening. Germans literally called end-of-day the "celebration evening." The entire language agrees: work ends, the party begins.
💡 Bonus: You can use "Feierabend!" as a full sentence when you finally close your laptop for the week. Alex will understand immediately.
