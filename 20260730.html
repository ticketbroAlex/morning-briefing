🎓 Daily Language School · Thursday Theme: Travel ✈️
🇩🇪
Su learns German
FERNWEH
[FERN-veh] · Neutrum
Longing for faraway places — wanderlust that physically hurts
DE: „Ich habe solches Fernweh — ich möchte einfach alles stehen und liegen lassen."
EN: "I have such wanderlust — I just want to drop everything and go."
🧠 Memory trick: Fern = far away + Weh = ache/pain → your heart literally hurts because you're not somewhere far away yet. Germans named the specific ache of not traveling. Very German of them.
Bonus: Gegenteil (opposite) = Heimweh [HAIM-veh] = homesickness. In Germany? Heimweh. In Brazil? Fernweh. You live in the exact gap between the two words.
🇧🇷
Alex learns Brazilian Portuguese
MOCHILÃO
[mo-shee-LOWN] · Substantivo masculino
An epic backpacking/road trip adventure — the big journey
PT: „A gente precisa fazer um mochilão pelo Nordeste um dia."
EN: "We need to do a big road trip through the Northeast one day."
🧠 Memory trick: Mochila = backpack + -ão = the BIG suffix. In Portuguese, -ão = supersized. Cão (dog) → Cãozão (massive dog). Mochila + ão = MASSIVE adventure. When you say mochilão, Brazilians immediately picture the vibe.
Bonus: Mochileiro/a = the person doing the mochilão (backpacker). "Ele é mochileiro" = he's a free-spirit traveler. Use it and watch Brazilians nod approvingly.

💑 Couple Tip · Thursday · Growth
The 10-Minute Growth Check-In
High-performance couples rarely make time to grow together — they grow in parallel. Tonight, share ONE thing each of you wants to improve personally in the next 30 days. Not work goals. Personal ones — patience, sleep, a habit, something small. Listen without offering solutions. Then pick one concrete way to support each other in it. No coaching, no fixing — just holding space.

The rule: the only acceptable response is "I see that. What would help?" Two people growing with each other, not just next to each other — that's the real compound interest.
Tonight after dinner: 10-minute growth check-in. One goal each. One support offer each. No phones.
